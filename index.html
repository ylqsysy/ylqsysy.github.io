About

I'm a Master's student in Surveying Engineering at Jiangsu Ocean University. Before this, I studied Data Science and Big Data Technology at Zhejiang A&F University (B.Sc., 2024).

My work sits between remote sensing and data science. I focus on satellite-based environmental monitoring and land cover change detection—combining multi-source Earth observation data with machine learning to track how landscapes shift over time.
