Profile

I am an M.Sc. candidate in Surveying Engineering at Jiangsu Ocean University. Before starting graduate study, I received a B.Sc. in Data Science and Big Data Technology from Zhejiang A&F University in 2024.

My research combines satellite remote sensing and geospatial data science for environmental monitoring. I focus on spectral-spatial representation learning for hyperspectral imagery and multi-temporal modeling of land cover change across multi-source Earth observation datasets.
