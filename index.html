About

I'm a Master's student in Surveying Engineering at Jiangsu Ocean University. Before this, I studied Data Science and Big Data Technology at Zhejiang A&F University (B.Sc., 2024).

My work sits between remote sensing and data science. I focus on satellite-based environmental monitoring and land cover change detection—combining multi-source Earth observation data with machine learning to track how landscapes shift over time.

Research

Remote Sensing & Environmental Analysis

I build processing workflows for multi-source satellite data—optical imagery, SAR, and their fusion—to map and monitor land surface change. The aim is to turn raw observations into reliable environmental indicators at regional scales.

Data-Driven Geospatial Methods

Drawing on my data science training, I apply statistical and machine learning techniques to geospatial problems: feature extraction from imagery, land cover classification, and spatiotemporal pattern modelling.
