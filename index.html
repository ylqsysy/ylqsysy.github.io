Profile

I am an M.Sc. candidate in Surveying Engineering at Jiangsu Ocean University. Before starting graduate study, I received a B.Sc. in Data Science and Big Data Technology from Zhejiang A&F University in 2024.

My research connects satellite remote sensing, geospatial data science, and deep sequence modeling. I work on hyperspectral image classification, multi-source Earth observation for environmental monitoring and land cover change, and spatiotemporal forecasting for urban traffic flow.
